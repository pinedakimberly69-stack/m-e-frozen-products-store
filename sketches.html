Store

Customer Flow (Lo‑fi)

[Landing/Products]
  - Header: Logo + Cart
  - Notice: "Mon–Sat 9–5"
  - Product grid:
     [Card] Name | Price | (+) Add | (+3)
     Limit hint (e.g., "Limit: 5")

[Cart]
  - Table: Product | Price | Qty (1..limit) | Line total | Remove
  - Totals: Subtotal, Total
  - CTA: Continue Shopping | Checkout

[Checkout]
  - Customer details: Name, Mobile, Email
  - Order type: Delivery / Pick-up
  - Address (if delivery)
  - Preferred Date & Time (today only, 09:00–17:00)
  - Payment: GCash | Bank | COD (Pickup)
  - Upload Proof (optional for non-COD)
  - Summary: itemized + total
  - CTA: Place Order (disabled outside window)

[Confirmation]
  - Thank-you message + order reference
  - Summary payload (for Sheets/API)
  - Back to Store

Business Rules (embedded in prototype)

  • Ordering window: Mon–Sat, 09:00–17:00 (Asia/Manila)
  • Quantity limits per product (Burger Patties: 5; Longganisa Garlic/Hamonado: 8)
  • Payment methods: GCash, Bank Transfer, Cash on Pick-up
  • Delivery/Pick-up choice + preferred same-day slot
